PRIVACY POLICY

Last Updated: November 2026

1. Zero Telemetry & Privacy First

Widget Project is engineered with total privacy at its foundation. We do not track, collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal identifiers, app analytics, or usage statistics to third parties.

2. On-Device Computation

All widget data, configuration states, custom formulas, and user-specified inputs are stored strictly on your local device and within your private iCloud container via Apple’s native CloudKit sandbox. We have no access to your data or credentials.

3. Weather Data & Apple WeatherKit

Weather data is powered directly through Apple WeatherKit. When location services are granted, your approximate location coordinates are handled directly by Apple's secure infrastructure to fetch local atmospheric conditions. We never store or log your location coordinates on any external server.

4. Music & Media Playback

Music widgets access Apple Music playback state locally using Apple MusicKit APIs on your device. No listening history or playlist tokens leave your personal device.

5. Inquiries & Customer Support

When you contact us through the Customer Support desk, information provided (such as your message and optional return email address) is used solely to respond to your technical question and resolve issues. It is never shared with third-party marketing services.
